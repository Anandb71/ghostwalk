### 1.2 Judge-tested demo heuristics (sources: https://blogs.reskilll.com/hackathon-demo-presentation-tips-pitch-3-minutes-win-2026/ , https://angelhack.com/blog/10-tips-to-help-you-rock-your-next-hackathon-demo/ , https://www.hackerearth.com/blog/10-tips-win-hackathon)
- Working live prototype > polished slides. Hard-code the demo path, pre-load data, mock nothing that is on the must-have list.
- One operator, one narrator. Record a backup video (also required: ≤5 min demo recording).
- Never show the editor/terminal. Every number on screen must be computed live from the real data (the brief forbids claimed improvements).

### 1.3 Cinematic-but-data-driven grammar (primitives that reliably produce the "lean forward")
1. **Before/after ghost**: the old state keeps running semi-transparent beside the new one, same input, divergent counters.
2. **Self-drawing path + ticking counter**: a route/line that draws itself while a number counts up (GSAP `DrawSVGPlugin` in 2D; growing `points` slice on drei `<Line>` in 3D; `motion`'s `animate(0, total)` for the counter).
3. **Event pulse**: disruption = red radial pulse (deck.gl `ScatterplotLayer` radius driven by `Date.now()`; three.js emissive + Bloom).
4. **Camera flight to evidence**: `camera-controls.setLookAt(...,true)` / `fitToBox(box,true)`; That Open `OrthoPerspectiveCamera.controls` is the same library.
5. **Heat memory**: floor/rack tiles accumulate visits → colour (InstancedMesh `instanceColor`).
6. **Threshold slider that re-flows everything live** (cap kW, cost ratio, confidence bar): the algorithm must be fast enough to run in a Web Worker at slider speed.
7. **Cascade of checks**: a test suite that turns green row-by-row with `motion` stagger — the honest-evaluation story rendered as theatre.

## 3. Per-problem wow moment (the achievable 30-second gasp, driven by real data + real algorithm)

### PS 4.2 Warehouse Digital Twin — "The Replay Race" (top pick)
**Moment (30 s):** Camera (drei `<CameraControls>` → `setLookAt(..., true)`) swoops from the packing dock down aisle 3 and rises to a 3/4 isometric. ~2,000 bins on 10 aisles are one `InstancedMesh` whose `instanceColor` is pick-frequency heat from the real month of UCI Online Retail II orders (cancelled "C" invoices and non-product codes removed in pandas). A picker (Kenney CC0 "Conveyor Kit" prop or a capsule — https://kenney-assets.itch.io/conveyor-kit, >60 industrial models, CC0) starts walking the aisle-graph shortest path for real invoice #581587; the path **draws itself** ahead of him (drei `<Line>` with a growing `points` slice, 3 px, cyan, Bloom), and the distance counter ticks in metres (`motion` `animate`). Judge drags "WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER" from the far rack to slot A1 (drei `<DragControls>` + snap). Press **Replay**: the ghost picker (same mesh, 35% opacity, wireframe) re-walks the *old* layout while the solid picker walks the *new* one on the same orders; counters diverge live ("Before 18.4 km · After 12.9 km · −30%"), floor tiles under both paths accumulate heat. A split KPI card then shows Original / Auto-baseline (fast movers nearest dock) / Player.
**Tech:** R3F 9.8.2 + drei 10.7.9 + three 0.186.1; `<Instances>`; aisle-graph Dijkstra in a Web Worker (replay of ~3k invoices computes in <100 ms; animate only the first N orders at 20× speed while totals come from the worker — state this on screen); `@react-three/postprocessing@3.2.1` Bloom + `n8ao` + SMAA with a quality toggle; `zustand` for layout state; static deploy on Vercel/Cloudflare Pages; data JSON pre-built by pandas. Stretch heat-map and leaderboard are already half-done by the instanceColor + localStorage.
**Why it gasps:** The 3D is the must-have, so every hour spent on visuals is also an hour on requirements, and the improvement is *measured on screen*, not claimed.

### PS 4.3 Last-Mile Route Planner — "Breakdown at 11:40"
**Moment:** Dark basemap (OpenFreeMap/CARTO) with Solomon R101-25 affine-mapped onto a 10 km city box (banner: "benchmark coordinates projected for visualisation"). ### PS 2.3 Unknown-Defect Steel Inspector — "The island nobody trained on"
**Moment:** A WebGL scatter (`regl-scatterplot@1.16.0`) of all 1,800 NEU-CLS embeddings (ResNet-18 fine-tuned on 5 classes via `timm==1.0.30`; UMAP computed offline with umap-learn) — five tight coloured clusters with dashed centroid radii, plus a grey, far-away island: the held-out class (e.g. *pitted surface*). Confusion matrix with an "unknown" row/column animates beside it; AUROC known-vs-unknown printed from the held-out split.
**Tech:** HF Spaces (Gradio or FastAPI+React) does embeddings on CPU in ~50 ms; optional "run on my laptop" mode with `@huggingface/transformers@4.3.1` `Xenova/dinov2-small` on WebGPU. Keep the 5 operator images out of all eval sets.
